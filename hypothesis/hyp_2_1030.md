# Hypothesis 2: raise the forward tracking reward to 3.0

## Claim

**Hypothesis:** Raising `forward_reward` from **2.5 to 3.0** will make the policy track faster forward commands more strongly and reduce the 2 m sprint time, while preserving all five finishes.

This is the next direct speed-tracking test. The existing `forward_reward = 2.5` runs both finished 5/5; their best local scores were 3.884 s (training seed 2) and 3.864 s (seed 3), both at checkpoint 1000. The seed 2 run is the clean-provenance comparison for this experiment.

## Change and controlled settings

Change only `params.forward_reward.value` in `src/microduck_challenges/sprint_2m/challenge.toml`:

```toml
[params.forward_reward]
value = 3.0
```

Keep all other environment and training settings unchanged: 4,096 environments, 3,000 maximum iterations, and training seed 2. Do not change `upright_reward`, `step_reward`, the command range, PPO settings, or the environment.

## Baseline and decision rule

- **Baseline:** `forward_reward = 2.5`, seed 2, best checkpoint 1000, **3.884 s**, **5/5 finishes**, winning command **0.70 m/s**.
- **Support the hypothesis:** a checkpoint from this run is at least **0.10 s faster** than 3.884 s on the same local evaluator and finishes **5/5**. Then verify the selected policy on the official Arena before calling it a leaderboard improvement.
- **Refute or leave inconclusive:** no 5/5 checkpoint reaches 3.784 s, or faster results come with a fall. Do not treat higher training reward alone as evidence of a faster sprint.

Score every available checkpoint and choose the best by 5/5 finishes first, then lowest time. The prior runs peaked around iteration 1000 and later checkpoints were often slower, so the final checkpoint is not presumed best.
